Referências

  • FASTQC — ANDREWS, S. FastQC: a quality control tool for high throughput sequence data. Babraham Bioinformatics, 2010. Disponível em: <https://www.bioinformatics.babraham.ac.uk/projects/fastqc/>. Acesso em: 15 out. 2025.
  • MULTIQC — EWELS, P.; MAGNUSSON, M.; LUNDIN, S.; KÄLLER, M. MultiQC: summarize analysis results for multiple tools and samples in a single report. Bioinformatics, 2016. Disponível em: <https://multiqc.info>. Acesso em: 15 out. 2025.
  • TRIM GALORE! — KRUEGER, F. Trim Galore! Wrapper tool around Cutadapt and FastQC to consistently apply quality and adapter trimming. Babraham Bioinformatics, 2012. Disponível em: <https://www.bioinformatics.babraham.ac.uk/projects/trim_galore/>. Acesso em: 15 out. 2025.
  • BWA-MEM — LI, H. Aligning sequence reads, clone sequences and assembly contigs with BWA-MEM. arXiv preprint arXiv:1303.3997, 2013. Disponível em: <https://arxiv.org/abs/1303.3997>. Acesso em: 15 out. 2025.
  • GATK (Genome Analysis Toolkit) — MCKENNA, A.; HANNA, M.; BANKS, E.; et al. The Genome Analysis Toolkit: a MapReduce framework for analyzing next-generation DNA sequencing data. Genome Research, 2010. Disponível em: <https://gatk.broadinstitute.org/>. Acesso em: 15 out. 2025.
  • PICARD — BROAD INSTITUTE. Picard Toolkit. Broad Institute, 2019. Disponível em: <https://broadinstitute.github.io/picard/>. Acesso em: 15 out. 2025.
  • SNPEFF — CINGOLANI, P.; et al. SnpEff: a program for annotating and predicting the effects of single nucleotide polymorphisms. Fly (Austin), 2012. Disponível em: <http://pcingola.github.io/SnpEff/>. Acesso em: 15 out. 2025.
  • SNPSIFT — CINGOLANI, P.; et al. SnpSift: a tool for filtering and manipulating VCF files. Frontiers in Genetics, 2012. Disponível em: <http://pcingola.github.io/SnpEff/>. Acesso em: 15 out. 2025.
  • R — R CORE TEAM. R: A language and environment for statistical computing. Vienna: R Foundation for Statistical Computing, 2023. Disponível em: <https://www.R-project.org/>. Acesso em: 15 out. 2025.
  • MAFTOOLS — MAYAKONDA, A.; et al. Maftools: efficient and comprehensive analysis of somatic variants in cancer. Genome Research, 2018. Disponível em: <https://bioconductor.org/packages/maftools/>. Acesso em: 15 out. 2025.

Bancos de Dados:

  • NCBI — reúne dados genéticos, proteicos e literários, incluindo GenBank e SRA.
  • ENSEMBL — fornece anotações genômicas detalhadas e comparativas entre espécies.
  • UCSC Genome Browser — permite visualizar genomas e suas anotações de forma interativa.
  • TCGA — disponibiliza dados genômicos e clínicos de diversos tipos de câncer.
  • GEO — armazena dados públicos de expressão gênica e experimentos funcionais (RNA-seq, microarray).
